## Extraction rules
1. ENTITY: if the subject is a named athlete → category "player"; if the school/team posted the stat → "team".
2. Take ONE hero stat (the number tied to the conference/rank claim). Ignore follow-up history sentences ("most since 2021…").
3. class_position = "Class | POS" from phrases like "Senior QB", "Junior WR", "Sophomore QB". Expand Fr/So/Jr/Sr to Freshman/Sophomore/Junior/Senior when clear.
4. rank = ONLY the ranking words (Tied for most / 2nd most / 3rd most / Most). Do not include conference or "this week" inside rank.
5. story_context = ONLY the trailing qualifier (conference / week / season / among group). Prefer short conference codes when natural: Sun Belt Conference → SBC, Southeastern Conference → SEC.
6. Do NOT copy the full CORE STORY into story_context.
7. Do NOT invent numbers, names, ranks, or scores. If no rank phrase exists, rank="" and story_context="" .
8. Title Case for stat_name and player_name (not ALL CAPS). rank may be Title Case like "Tied for most" or "2nd most".
9. Drop dates like "on Sept. 5" from outputs.
